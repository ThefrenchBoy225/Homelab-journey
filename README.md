# 🏠 Homelab Journey

Documenting my hands-on journey building a home lab to develop practical IT and cybersecurity skills — as I work toward breaking into the field without a traditional IT degree.

## About Me
I hold a **CompTIA Security+** certification and am actively building practical, hands-on experience through a self-built home lab, targeting entry-level roles in **IT Support / Help Desk** and **Cybersecurity**.

## Why This Repo
Certifications prove knowledge — this repo proves I can actually *do* the work. Every entry documents something I built, broke, and fixed, including the real troubleshooting (not just the parts that worked perfectly the first time).
